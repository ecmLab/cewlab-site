Prof. Tu is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Rochester
Institute of Technology. He was previously a postdoctoral researcher in the group of Prof. Gerbrand
Ceder at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. His research solves electro-chemo-mechanical
problems in energy storage systems — such as solid-state batteries — and in water desalination
systems. Throughout his PhD and postdoc he collaborated closely with experimentalists and theorists
at both LBNL and UC Berkeley across a range of research areas, and he continues to pair modeling
with in-house experiment at RIT.

## Education & appointments

**2021 – present** · **Rochester Institute of Technology**
Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering.

**2017 – 2021** · **Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory**
Postdoctoral Researcher, Materials Science Division. Supervisor: Gerbrand Ceder.
Electro-chemo-mechanics of Li metal and composite cathodes in solid-state batteries.

**2012 – 2017** · **University of California, Berkeley**
PhD in Mechanics and Environmental Engineering. Supervisor: Shaofan Li.
Nano-micro mechanics of 2D-material membranes for water purification and desalination.
